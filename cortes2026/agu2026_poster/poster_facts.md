Conventions. "1 bar" = the run's first kept ascending ambient step (P < 3 MPa; its actual pressure is in the CSV, 0.06-1.7 MPa). sigma values are ANCHORED (1-bar step set to McCleskey 2011/2012), as in the main figure. "sigma(200 MPa)" = linear interpolation between the two compression steps that bracket 200 MPa, or, when the highest step is 0.0-3 MPa below 200, the line through the last two compression steps extended to 200 (method column in the CSV). "Up/down" = difference of sigma/sigma(1 bar) between decompression and compression at the same pressure (decompression pressure; compression interpolated), 20-190 MPa, percentage points of the 1-bar value. "Step range" = half-width of the pct increase using the min/max sweep of each step.

## g. Data-quality caveats

Plain summary. Absolute conductivities are anchored: each run is rescaled so its first 1-bar step equals prior 1-bar data (McCleskey 2011/2012), which keeps the measured pressure dependence but replaces the run's own calibration; the rescaling factors range from -9.8% to +5.5% (Aug 2025 0.5 mol/kg: -9.8% to +5.5%; Nov 17-19: +0.0% to +4.4%; KCl: -1.9% to +0.5%). After the Nov 2026 connection repair the cell constant changed (KCl standard 120.62 -> 115.21 1/m, QUOTED) and the 1-bar NaCl readings sit below McCleskey (2011) by an amount that grows on cooling (QUOTED -2.7% to -6.8% with Diaz's K; recomputed below); its cause is not established. The 16.2 C anchor step may not have been thermally settled. The percent increase to 200 MPa does not depend on the anchoring, but could depend on the offset's cause (bound below). Details:

**Anchoring.** Every run is multiplied by one factor so its first ascending 1-bar step equals McCleskey (2011), or McCleskey et al. (2012) where T < 5 C. The factors (reference/measured) are: Aug 2025 0.5 mol/kg 0.902 to 1.055 (-9.8% to +5.5%); Oct 7-8 and Nov 16 exactly 1 by construction of their cell constants (K = 122.56 and 120.36 1/m; Oct 7-8 uses the Oct 14 KCl estimate); Nov 17-19 1.000 to 1.044 (+0.0% to +4.4%) with ONE K = 120.093 1/m (Nov 17 24.8 C = 1 by construction); KCl 0.70 mol/kg 0.981 to 1.005 (-1.9% to +0.5%, pre-repair K = 120.62 1/m). The measured pressure dependence is kept; the ratio is unchanged. The anchor uncertainty used in error bars (1% MC2011, 2% MC12) is an assumption, not derived. **Post-repair 1-bar offset (QUOTED from open-questions 2026-11-20 and recomputed here with Diaz's K = 116.84):** with that K the Nov 17-19 NaCl readings fall below McCleskey (2011) by -2.7% at 24.8 C growing to -6.8% at 7.0 C (recomputed: 24.8 C: -2.7%, 20.6 C: -3.5%, 16.3 C: -3.7%, 11.5 C: -4.9%, 7.0 C: -6.8%, 1.7 C: -5.6%). With the Nov 19 KCl standard K = 115.21 (QUOTED) the offsets would be -4.1 to -8.1%. **Cell constant across the repair:** KCl standard K = 120.62 1/m before (Nov 5-12, computed from the CSV) vs 115.21 after (QUOTED) a change of -4.5%; the Nov 17-19 NaCl series uses K = 120.093 (self-calibrated on the 24.8 C 1-bar step vs McCleskey), +4.2% away from the post-repair standard value, so the absolute scale of the Nov 17-19 data rests on that choice and on the anchoring.
